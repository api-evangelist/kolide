When workers bring their own equipment on the job, it opens the door to all kinds of trouble–just ask the NFL. In 2006, the NFL instituted a rule change that allowed each team’s offense to use their own footballs during games, as long as those footballs met the league’s requirements. This change was made at the request of the league’s quarterbacks, who wanted more control over the equipment they use to throw touchdowns.
