The first known security incident involving a compromised device occurred during the Bronze Age, in present-day Turkey. In that case, the Trojan guards were good men, not bad actors with malicious intent. But they made a fatal mistake when they failed to inspect the large, horse-shaped device they dragged inside the city gates.
