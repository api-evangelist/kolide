In 1968, Robert Probst, research director for office furniture company Herman Miller, released an office design called the “Action Office II.” It was the culmination of almost a decade studying the psychological core of the corporate workspace. The Action Office II would provide privacy while still fostering communication. It would encourage workers to move more freely and adapt their space to their needs.
