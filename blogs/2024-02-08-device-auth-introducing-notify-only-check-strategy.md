Note: Notify Only is a feature only available for organizations using our Device Trust for Okta solution . Blocking devices from authenticating can feel intimidating, whether you’re rolling out Kolide’s Device Trust capability for the first time, or just enabling a new Check. Now, rather than jumping right into the deep-end, you can dip your toes into the water of device trust and more gently roll out Checks with our new remediation strategy: Notify Only .
