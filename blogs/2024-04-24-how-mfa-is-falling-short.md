In baseball, it’s tempting to think that once you’re on a base, the hard part is over. But then, just when you think you’re safe (you are literally “safe”) the baseman hits you with the hidden ball trick . Your opponent appears to throw the ball away, but merely hides it and tags you in the moment you’re most vulnerable.
