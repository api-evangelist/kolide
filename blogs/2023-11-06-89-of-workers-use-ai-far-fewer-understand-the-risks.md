In late November 2022, OpenAI released ChatGPT to the public and launched the AI revolution. A few days later, Kolide’s CEO took to our company’s Slack and urged us to try out this new “society changing technology.” Around the world, bosses were telling their employees the same thing: start playing with generative AI and figure out how it can make you (and us) faster, smarter, better. Less than a year later, that advice has taken hold to an astonishing degree.
